10 ATS Tips for UAE Job Applications

  1. 1Use standard section headingsStick to “Work Experience”, “Education”, “Skills” — ATS parsers look for these exact labels.
  2. 2Avoid tables and columnsMulti-column layouts and tables confuse parsers and scramble your data.
  3. 3Include keywords from the job descriptionMirror the exact skills and titles the employer lists.
  4. 4Use standard fonts (Arial, Calibri)Decorative fonts may not render or parse correctly.
  5. 5Save as PDF, not WordPDF preserves layout and is widely ATS-compatible.
  6. 6Include UAE phone format (+971)Use the full international format so recruiters can reach you.
  7. 7Add your LinkedIn profile URLA complete profile builds trust and adds keywords.
  8. 8Quantify achievements with numbers“Increased sales 30%” beats “responsible for sales”.
  9. 9Keep to 1-2 pages maxUAE recruiters skim — be concise and relevant.
  10. 10Use active verbs (Managed, Led, Achieved)Strong verbs signal impact and ownership.
